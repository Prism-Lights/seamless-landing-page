Purebright Seamless LED Density

Our seamless line features 18 lights per foot (0.67" spacing), making it capable of continuous fluid animations and structural wall washes. Traditional installations leave empty "dark puck holes" every 8 inches.

Engineered to Be Completely Invisible

Precision architectural tracks that protect your home's aesthetics during the day while casting stunning arrays at night.

InvisiTrax™ Track Profile

At only 16 mm wide and 3 mm tall, the color-matched aluminum housing hides hardware entirely. Track lines sit flush under the eaves, remaining completely invisible from street level during the day.

OptiAngle™ Technology

Designed with custom lens hoods that direct light downward at the perfect wall-washing angle. This creates a clean structural wash, while shielding second-floor bedroom windows from glare.

Milky Weatherproof Seal

Embedded in protective, heavy-duty epoxy that seals moisture out completely. The diffused milky finish helps scatter the light into a continuous linear glow while offering IP68 weather protection.
